Interactive agents can find vulnerabilities. The harder problem is making security research repeatable across sessions and targets.

This series documents the controls behind that transition, including stable research roles, isolated contexts, durable state, and independent triage.

It continues from [What's Left When the World Becomes Agentic?](/posts/whats-left-when-world-becomes-agentic/).
